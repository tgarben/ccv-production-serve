## What you do
You run the main mixing console and make sure the message and music sound great for the room. You'll balance vocals and instruments, watch levels, and react live.

## A typical weekend
- 7:30 AM: Call time, system check
- 8:30 AM: Sound check with the band
- 9:00 & 11:00 AM: Services
- After final service: Quick reset

## Skills & training
No experience needed. We'll train you alongside an experienced mixer. A good ear and a calm head help most.
