## What you do
You let the band and speakers know when to stand by and when to load. You bring out the podium or mic stands when they're needed, and you help check and troubleshoot everyone's batteries.

## Skills & training
A hands-on position: you'll be on stage, moving gear and working closely with the band and speakers. More involved than Lyrics & Slides, and less demanding than Video Director.
